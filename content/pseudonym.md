A [[pseudonym]] is a fake or false name that a person selects for themselves, often, for [[representational]] purposes.

For example, [[Richard Bachman]] is a [[pseudonym]] of [[Stephen King]].

Importantly, [[Malcolm X]] is not a [[pseudonym]] for [[Malcolm Little]] nor is [[Amiri Baraka]] for [[Leroi Jones]] or [[Molefi Kete Asante]] for [[Arthur Lee Smith Jr]].

[[Fernando Pessoa]], the author of *The Book of Disquiet*, wrote under many different names, but he called them [[heteronyms]].
