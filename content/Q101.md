Outside of [[CD Trader]] in [[Lombard]], the primary place I got music from was [[Q101]] and the Helen Plum [[library]]. 

I used to make [[mixtapes]], recording directly off the radio or cd.

I think I even called into the station once, but I can't remember.

I recall staying up late listening to Dr. Drew and Adam Carolla on Loveline (look how far both of them have fallen from such heights). They used to be funny and helpful. Crazy.

On [[YouTube]], I always make a [[playlist]] for Q101, and, almost always the first two songs added are [[Geggy Tah]] - Whoever You Are and [[Local H]] - Bound for the Floor. I don't know why, but when I think of Q101, I think of those two songs. I feel they almost encapsulate the breadth and depth of the [[alternative]] music movement.
