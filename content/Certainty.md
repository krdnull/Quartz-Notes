What's the [[cliche]]? That the only thing certain in life is that life is uncertain. 

In the "west", [[Heraclitus]] said it first.
I was born in the "west", so he is who I know when it comes to attribution.
I came to him through [[Heidegger]], and I came to him because of whoever designed the cover to *Being and Time*.

We can't help what our eye is drawn to, especially as a child (I was only 12), but we are attracted to what we are attracted to. It can be entrained, but we are often constrained by the world that we were [[thrown]] into.

There was a world before us, just as there will be a world after us.

In point of fact, there is only one world - yours, and it is only the borders and bounds which are [[fuzzy]] as that is the threshold where intra transforms into inter.

Where am I to go?
