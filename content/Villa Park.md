[[Villa Park]] is the town next to [[Lombard]].

Even though, Lombard is my hometown, I spent considerably more time in Villa Park.

Villa Park, for me, was largely split into three sections.

Section 1 is centered around [[Roosevelt]] Road.

Section 2 is centered around two schools: [[Jackson]] Junior High and [[Willowbrook]] High School.

Section 3 is centered around the [[Prairie Path]].

Villa Park is the perfect [[skateboard]] town for someone at my level: curbs, manual pads, and small stair sets.

It has a perfect [[loop]]. I would start at the Baker's Square ollieing over the parking blocks, cruise a bit and ollie the stair set, power slide to slalom down the incline, ollie the sewer grate into the sidewalk bank, and then push as fast as I could through the Burlington Coat Factory lot hitting the curbs along the way until I hit the manual pad where I pulled out my tech tricks. Once down there, a quick jot over to the post office to ollie some gaps, and then onto Willowbrook where I would spend the rest of my time on the absolutely perfect manual pads.
