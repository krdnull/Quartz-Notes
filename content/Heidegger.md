We can't help who we read first, as who we read first, whether we like it or not, especially given [[hermeneutics]], will affect everything that is read after that. 

For me, [[Heidegger]] is who I happen to read first, and it was because of the [[aesthetics]] of the design of the dust jacket to *Being and Time*. 

Black fill with the title laid bare and plain in white - in stark contrast. [[Minimalism]] before I knew the word. I couldn't look away; it had a gravitational pull, and compared to the books in its environs, in its surround - it stood out. It couldn't not be seen, and so that it how it became first.

This happened at the Helen Plum [[library]] in [[Lombard]] in [[1995]]. I was 12. 

In 1995, I also saw [[Hackers]], which is my favorite movie. A movie I've seen well over 300 times - I [[loop]] things.

Hackers led me to the word [[halcyon]], as the main theme of Hackers was composed by Orbital: Halcyon on and on.
