Inside, I feel [[hollow]].

It is why I refer to myself as a [[cipher]], but I'm not a cipher - not if I am [[honest]].

I know what I am, but I never say because to write it is a step too far. It is one thing to [[know]] and another thing to know. Only one of those scenarios has plausible deniability, or to put it another way - allows one to excuse, to [[defer]].

Once a word has been spoken, it cannot be unspoken, but until that moment, like light, it exists in all possible worlds, and only at the point of measurement does it [[collapse]] into a particle or a wave. The [[cliche]]: the cat isn't dead until one opens the box. Prior to the opening, there is only [[probability]].

This is about a [[hope]], a [[dream]], a [[choice]], a [[risk]].
