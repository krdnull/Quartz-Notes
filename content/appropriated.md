Title: Appropriated

\---



Appropriation, in art, but also in general, is making use of material that already exists and putting it forth as your own.

\[\[Marcel Duchamp]] most famously, or infamously, did by in the creation of his work *Fountain*, which was a common, normal, public urinal. This was then famously, or infamously, reappropriated by \[\[Sherrie Levine]].

In the case of \[\[Lillian Budd]], \[\[et al]] not only used her name, but also her work, specifically *April Snow*, and presented it to the world as her own.

The argument which lies at the heart of appropriation revolves around \[\[context]] and \[\[originality]].

Interculturally, cultures which are more attuned with achievement tend to take a dim view of the arguments in favor of appropriation.
