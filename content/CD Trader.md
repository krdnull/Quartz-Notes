CD Trader was the name of the [[record store]] located in [[Lombard]].

This easily the store I spent the most time in when I was young. That, or Unicorn, a [[comic book]] shop in [[Villa Park]].

They were both run by the nicest folks, so caring, so kind, so welcoming, and that meant a lot to a kid like me. There isn't much more important than those [[third spaces]]. The Helen Plum [[library]] also filled a similar function.

It was at CD Trader that I truly become an omnivorous eclectic when it came to music. I truly do like it all, and I've yet to hear music that I couldn't find something in it that was worth listening to.

The two most formative albums that i got there was Lorin Maazel conducting the Wiener Philharmoniker in Symphony No. 1 composed by Gustav [[Mahler]], and, the other: [[Wolverine]] Blues by [[Entombed]].
