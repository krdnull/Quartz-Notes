My obsession with the [[road]] began with the [[English]] class I took at [[Willowbrook]] with Michael [[Sullivan]]. The idea of the class was based on a book by Douglas [[Brinkley]] called *The Majic Bus*, which detailed a living classroom, as the class, quite literally, travelled through the [[United States]]. Sullivan sought to imitate this in kind by having us create our own [[maps]] of the United States. 

I do not recall all of mine, of course, as that is over a [[generation]] ago, but I do remember [[Bob Dylan]] playing a key role as well as the towns of [[Duluth]] and [[Echo]] which are both in [[Minnesota]].
