[[Arcanum]] has been my favorite [[video game]] ever since I first played it back in the early 2000s.. I actually recall buying it in the box from a used computer store I used to go to on the regular. I don't recall how I found the shop, or why I went there on a regular basis, as it doesn't seem like the kind of place that would warrant multiple visits, but I do recall visiting it on the regular. 

I think I bought a few laptops there, and a few more [[video games]].

I bought Arcanum without knowing anything about it at all. Not dissimilar to how I came across *Being and Time* by [[Heidegger]]. The aesthetics drew me in, and they have stood the test of [[time]], perhaps, that is they key? 

Faith? 
Trust?
