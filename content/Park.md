The [[park]] was in [[Lombard]].
It was the place of two [[lagoons]] and willow trees.

The willow tree weeps for no reason.
If a reason is to die.

That is all I recall from the first poem I wrote.
I wasn't yet 12.

The park was actually two parks.
Park, lagoon, lagoon, park.
The second lagoon and park also had a [[forest]] and the [[portal]].
The second lagoon had a basketball court and a baseball diamond.
Neither of which I ever saw in use.
I used the court for [[skateboarding]].
Both parks had playgrounds.

At one point we "tagged" an electrical box with our "crews" [[name]]: 630
6 aligned left with the 3 aligned right and placed level with the 6 and then the Zero encircling both. We'd "tag" all our spots such. There was a few of us, but the only name I remember is Neal. He had ginger hair and was tall. He lived in a nice house up the block, and he was older than I was. I skateboarded and he aggressively inlineed. I used to film our crew. I wonder what happened to those tapes. I'd love to see those now. I'd love to see what we thought was worthy to [[remember]].
