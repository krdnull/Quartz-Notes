Gregor [[Weichbrodt]] published a book in 2016 entitled: *Dictionary of non-notable artists*, which was composed of a collection of artists deemed non-notable by [[Wikipedia]].

Lawrence B. [[Sawyer]], the father of modern [[internal auditing]], does not have a [[Wikipedia]] page. He is mentioned eleven times on the [[Wikipedia]] page for [[Internal Audit]].

In 1951, Hans [[Keller]] in a review of Benjamin [[Britten]] and Peter [[Pears]] performance of *Die schone Mullerin* composed by Franz [[Schubert]], wrote: "or to try to find a metaphor for the perfectly shaped [[unnotability]] of the piano's notes in background motion, as in the sextuplets of *Wohin?* or in the semiquavers of *Eifersucht und Stolz*" (Mitchell & Keller, 1981, p. 203).
