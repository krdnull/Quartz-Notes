There were two [[lagoons]] near the house I grew up in [[Lombard]], [[Illinois]].
There were fish in it.
Catfish, blue grill, crappie.
The second lagoon was the one near the [[forest]] and the [[portal]].
The second lagoon was the one that had the willow trees.
The first lagoon is the one we walked around to get to the second lagoon.
I wonder why that was.
Sometimes, i would go to my friend's grandma's house, Ryan and his brother Brennan, I think, and that allowed us to take a shortcut that allowed us tot skip the first lagoon altogether.
We were friends, I think, by circumstance of location, although, we did stay friends for a time after they moved to [[Villa Park]].
We simply grew apart, as we became interested in different things. I think they thought I was immature - they probably weren't wrong. 
I'm still immature when it comes to the things that I like.
At one point, I was probably labeled a bad influence.
That was also true, and I do hope that I didn't mislead them if that was the case.
My memory is not as good as it used to be, and I am only getting older.
Eventually, I suspect I will no longer remember - just as I've forgotten most of the people I went to school with. 
I wonder how many I could name, more importantly, I wonder how many I can envision, and I wonder how that vision lines up with what they look like now.
I've not seen any of them in so long.
