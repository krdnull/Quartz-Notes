I used to think that [[Enchanted Castle]] existed elsewhere, outside of [[Lombard]], [[Illinois]]. I mean it seems like the kind of place that would have been franchised back in the 1990s. I mean it had [[animatronics]]! It was only later that I learned that this experience was unique to the place I was born. It really does demonstrate the absolute [[arbitrariness]] of life, and how easy it is to fall into the trap that everyone had a similar [[experience]] of life as you? You begin to [[wonder]] what else was [[accidental]], what else was [[incidental]], what else have I taken for granted? In the end, in the grand scheme, of course, [[nothing]] matters, as when we die, we carry [[nothing]]. Life only matters to the living, and while you think you care not what the living thing - the dead, in fact, do not - they are dead - they think nothing, are nothing.
