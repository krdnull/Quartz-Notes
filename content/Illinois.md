Leaving [[Lombard]] via the [[Prairie Path]] you will run through the towns of [[Villa Park]], [[Elmhurst]], [[Berkeley]], and [[Bellwood]].

In [[Villa Park]], you stop at [[Unicorn]], a [[comic book]] shop, situated next to the Villa Park Historical Society and the old [[Ovaltine]] factory right off the Prairie Path.

In [[Elmhurst]], I would get off the path on Spring Road, take a left without crossing the street, and head toward a [[Magic the Gathering]] card shop whose name has slipped my mind (it was almost three decades ago).

[[Berkeley]] was a [[skatethrough]].

[[Bellwood]] was the end of the line, whereupon I started the long skate back to [[Lombard]].
