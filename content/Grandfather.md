My [[grandfather]] wanted to be Robert [[Bly]], but was actually Bly's father - in that he [[abdicated]].

My grandfather, as a young man, wanted to be an engineer, but became a union electrician out of necessity - he had five children in a relatively short period of time after getting married young, at least young from my perspective.

My grandfather returned to college at one point, and took an introductory [[philosophy]] class which was focused on existentialism. They read Albert [[Camus]], and he would continual regale me with the tale when his instructor complimented him on his insight into [[Sisyphus]].

I became who I think my grandfather wanted to become, at least when it came education. I wonder, sometimes, if he was [[happy]] with his choice. I wonder even more if he would be [[honest]] in answering such a question. 

How much is your [[dream]] worth?
A dangerous [[question]].
