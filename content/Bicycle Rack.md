The [[bicycle rack]] is (or was) located at [[Enchanted Castle]] in [[Lombard]], [[Illinois]]. That is where I got my first major injury, the breaking of my left wrist from [[skateboarding]]. The break was misdiagnosed as a sprain, and eventually my wrist got locked in a rigid, straight position. I had to get the bone rebroke in the 1990s, and they discovered that the bone had disintegrated, and so I had to get a bone from my hip to replace the one in my wrist so that I could regain functionality - currently, and for the foreseeable future, I have about 65 to 70 percent movement. Nothing was ever done to the doctor who was forced to retire due to Alzheimer's, if I am recalling correctly. The injury happened during the winter and the bicycle rack was icy.
