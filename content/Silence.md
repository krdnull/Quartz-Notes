The \[\[rhetoric]] of \[\[silence]] is said to have started with the work of Max \[\[Picard]] in 1952.

Charles \[\[Courtenay]] wrote *The Empire of Silence* in 1916.

In a review of *The Empire of Silence* in *The Expository Times* said that Charles \[\[Courtenay]] "missed, we think, that very striking confession of Dr. R.W. Dale which we are glad to see quoted in Professor Jackson's new anthology" (1917. p. 303).

The book by Professor Jackson is *Leaves of Healing* was published in 1916. It is a daily \[\[devotional]] \[\[anthology]], and the piece included by R.W. \[\[Dale]] is about the \[\[rhetoric]] of \[\[silence]].

Indeed, in 1898, A.W.W. Dale, his son, published *The Life of R.W. Dale of Birmingham*, wherein he covered, extensively, his father's interest in the \[\[rhetoric]] of \[\[silence]].
