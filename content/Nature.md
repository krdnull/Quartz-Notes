[[Churchill Woods]] was the closest [[nature]] preserve to [[Lombard]], or,  at least, that was the one that I visited the most. I had to drive there to walk. 

The [[Prairie Path]] I could walk to, but that was a different kind of nature, one infested by humanity as it was carved into the [[suburban]] [[landscape]]. 

The Churchill Woods had white-tailed deer and blue and grey herons, and foliage that I cannot name. 

Most of the paths there were laid out, but there were a few paths, less trodden, that could also be walked without too must disruption of the environs, of the surround. 

It's primary feature was a picturesque bridge over the waters - straight out of a greeting card. The other water feature was a [[waterfall]] of sorts, right next to the roadway, where people would stop and [[fish]].

The waters had the appearance of a series of connected lakes but was more of a marsh/reservoir that bled and fed into a [[river]] [[system]].
