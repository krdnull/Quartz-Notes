I attended [[Jackson]] Junior High for a year, I think. When I was there I played the [[trombone]] in both the orchestra as well as the [[jazz]] band. I was in both because there, unsurprisingly, were not many trombone players not because of my talent, although, I was not bad at the trombone, but I was, at best, a second chair, and the jazz band was meant for those who had talent.

In jazz band, we did not really play jazz, at least not jazz as I understand it now, but probably jazz as it was understood by someone with [[suburban]] sensibilities.  What I recall, was that the percussion section, who had a shockingly large number who could also play electric bass and guitar, used to play whatever was current on [[Q101]]. 

Jazz band is where I first heard Smells Like Teen Spirit by [[Nirvana]], off their Nevermind album, which I consider the greatest album ever, as it is the only album that I knew all the song lyrics even before I secured my own copy of the release. 

I, of course, picked up my copy at [[CD Trader]] in [[Lombard]], my one-stop [[record store]].

Such [[halcyon]] days.
