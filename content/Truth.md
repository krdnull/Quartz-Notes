I want the [[truth]] even if it hurts. It is similar to my [[memory]]. I am not just trying to [[remember]] the good things; I am trying to remember everything because I know if I don't remember everything I won't be able to remember the thing that I am trying to remember whether that be in the past, present, future, or outside of [[time]] altogether.

I know I will feel [[shame]] as I remember things - that is unavoidable because I have done things that are worthy of shame. Plus, there is no [[glory]] without shame. Even Pilgrim had to make it through the slough to reach the celestial hill.
