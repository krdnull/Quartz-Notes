The [[comic book]] character NOT the movie character.

I have always felt a strong affinity for Wolverine. Not only are we similar in height and body; I also had an affinity for his attitude - his anger, his ferocity, his rage alongside his determination, his endurance, his perseverance.

Similarly, I related strongly to the dwarves in the [[video game]] [[Arcanum]]: Of Steamworks and Magick Obscura, specifically Gudmund Ore Bender, alone in the abandoned mines of the Black Mountain Clan, screaming the truth into the abyss, into the void - I [[scream]] still.
