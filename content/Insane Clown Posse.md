I was introduced to the Insane Clown Posse by someone who lived on Edson Street in [[Lombard]], [[Illinois]]. Sadly, I can no longer recall their name - it was back in 1997, as the new album was The Great Milenko (one of the few albums I still know all the lyrics of, even though I've not listened to it in some time).

I think the Insane Clown Posse is meant to be a guilty pleasure, but I simply find them pleasurable - no guilt, no shame, but I really only know about the music side of things, and I know nothing (much) about the juggalo [[subculture]].

This only came to mind because I was on Wikipedia and saw they released an album in 2025: The Naught, and I had to give it an immediate listen, and it was a fantastic experience. 

I think one day I will listen to their albums back-to-back-back, as it is rare, I think, to see a band age and mature over time. Maybe, I am feeling a bit wistful, as I just watched Dead Meat's podcast on Phantasm 5, which was a brilliant, from my point of view, of that series - I don't want to spoil anything, but if you loved Phantasm 1, you'll really appreciate Phantasm 5 - it really was for the creator, the actors, actresses, and the fans.
