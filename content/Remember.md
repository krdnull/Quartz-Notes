I do not know exactly what I am trying to [[remember]]. I know that I am trying to remember something, but I don't know what it is. I am convinced that it will emerge if I collect enough nodes and vectors for my [[network]]. As the clusters grow, so does the density.

At some point, I no longer know exactly when, I destroyed my [[trombone]]. I now wish I hadn't, but I did, and I know that not only did I harm myself - I harmed others, but I don't know who they are - I probably ghosted them, as that is what I used to do, or, perhaps, they ghosted me once they saw who I was at the time - I definitely wasn't someone that anyone would want to be around.

For the [[truth]] to emerge, you need to remember both the good and the bad. I think people have forgotten that. I think [[social media]] has warped that, which is why I think we have so many adults with stunted emotional growth. Part of growing is not only an acknowledgment of your failures, but of what actions you have taken in [[rectification]].

I think sometimes people think I want to go back - back to [[Lombard]], for example, but I don't want to go back. My remembering may be related to the past, but it also could be related to the present or the future. You have to be open to the possibility. You never know. You can never be sure.

There is no [[shame]] in falling, there is only shame in not learning from the fall.
