[[Necropolitics]] is a theory regarding the ultimate [[disposability]] of the human body, in short or in sum, the inverse or converse of [[biopolitics]] placing the focus on death and drying rather than life and living.

[[Achille Mbembe]] formulated this theory in early 2000s building and expanding on the work of [[Michel Foucault]] who had theorized [[biopolitics]].

[[Alberto Ciria]], a [[Peron]] scholar, in the early 1980s formulated a theory of [[necropolitics]] "that rejoiced in the glorification of the past, and at times even a cult of martyrdom and death" (1989, p. 309).
