When I was younger, and even not so young, I used to love making [[lists]]. 

I made lists of everything. Of [[books]], I wanted to read, [[music]] I wanted to hear, [[video games]] I wanted to play.

Lists upon lists upon lists.

Ironically, it is when I need list the most that I stopped making them.

What got in the way?

Was it [[time]]?

Something happened in the intervening - the advent of the information superhighway.

The lists became [[bookmarks]], then they became instantly gratified.

The making of the list was gratifying, and in the delay of [[gratification]] amplified and intensified the gratification when an item on the list was satisfied.

I need to become okay with [[boredom]] again.
