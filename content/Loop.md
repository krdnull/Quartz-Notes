I [[loop]] some things. I loop [[music]]. I sometimes will loop [[movies]] and [[video games]], but I don't think I've ever looped a book, in fact, with [[books]] I tend to do the opposite of looping - one and done, which isn't to say that I don't reread books - I have reread books, but I tend to only rerread books in part, not in whole, because I am rereading the book for a specific reason, normally, to make sure that what I recalled of it was accurate. I trust my [[memory]], perhaps, too much.

The looping music, movies, and videos games trod down the neural paths, and the books track and tramp over the trodden down ground. These things, I feel, work in tandem. Not everything can be looped, it has to give off a certain feel, almost [[nostalgia]] without being nostalgic. Actual nostalgia is a [[pathology]], but a semblance of it has a [[sweet]] sense of [[desire]], as Robert [[Bly]] intimated. We used to desire god, but god is dead, and so we have to replace god with something else, as if you don't, your life, likely, will become [[hollow]] as there is no [[point]].

Antonio [[Machado]], who I came to through Bly, said that the creation of something new isn't the be all to end all, instead, your true aim is to be your brother's keeper, maybe, just maybe, that is my answer, my [[goal]].
