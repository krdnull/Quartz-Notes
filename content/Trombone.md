The reason I played the [[trombone]] was because I got it for free from my great-[[grandfather]] who, I think, at one point, played the trombone for [[jazz]]. 

I know he had a vinyl collection like a [[record store]]. I think those were all lost in a flood, but I do recall a lot of Jack Teagarden and jazz from what would have been his young years.

I destroyed that trombone and another in a fit of rage. I do not know why, and I am shamed by those actions now, and they cannot be undone, so the [[shame]] persists.

I do love trombone music still.

I forgot why my grandparents made me join band. Maybe they thought it would help me make friends, as I was (and am) fairly misanthropic, or they thought it would be good for me by building [[character]], who knows? I don't.
