Lillian [[Budd]] was a Swedish-American author. Her most famous book is *April Snow*, but the book I know her for is *Footsteps on the Tall Grass Prairie* which is a history of my hometown,[[Lombard]], [[Illinois]].

[[et al]] (Merylyn [[Tweedie]]) is a New Zealand artist collective. They most famously, or infamously, were selected to represent New Zealand at the 2005 Venice Biennale. At some point, [[et al]] [[appropriated]] the name and work of Lillian [[Budd]], presenting and publishing, for a time, under that [[pseudonym]]
